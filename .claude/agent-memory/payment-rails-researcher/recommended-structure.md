# Recommended product/legal structure (decided in research, 2026-06)

Two-track plan for a solo Canada-based dev:

1. **Now / default:** Run **non-redeemable "social" tokens** (Variant 2) — sell tokens for real money, but they **never cash out** (implement NO `withdrawal` path). This keeps you OUT of gambling law AND FINTRAC money-transmitter law, so it's launchable solo today on a mainstream PSP. Monetize **token sales + cosmetics + subscriptions**, not rake. Make tokens **non-transferable** (a secondary cash market re-triggers "prize of value").
2. **Later / real money:** Cash wagering only via a **licensed** route — realistically **Ontario** (or **Alberta**, launching 2026-07-13) as a **B2B supplier or operator partner**, not a solo direct-licensed book.

**Avoid:** the **sweepstakes/dual-currency** model (collapsing — 8+ US state bans in 2026, California extended criminal liability to processors; untested/risky in Canada) and **Kahnawake** licensing (offshore-style, bans US traffic, no provincial legitimacy).

**Crown-jewel asset:** the **parimutuel Live-betting engine** is both the most defensible product (no house outcome risk) and the most viable **B2B-licensable** asset for the Ontario partner route. **Crypto stays optional and last** — fiat (CAD) regulated rail first.
